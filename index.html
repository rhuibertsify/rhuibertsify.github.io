Introduction and our vision for Wynwood

In synergy with our neighbors

WOMA sits next to the Bakehouse Arts Complex. Together they form a creative pair that extends the creative axis along NW 5th Avenue. The two projects share priorities, from artists’ housing and cultural programming to green space and community facilities, and they strengthen each other and Wynwood Norte.

preserving the neighborhood scale

WOMA is designed as a vertical village. The building is split into stacked blocks of different sizes, each with its own color and façade pattern, so it reads as a cluster of smaller buildings. This keeps it in line with the open, green and low-rise character of its surroundings.

Working towards a resilient neighborhood

what we have been working on…

The Resilient Neighborhood:Wynwood & Wynwood Norte

Wynwood and Wynwood Norte are undergoing a rapid transformation.

At the same time, the neighborhood also faces environmental, social and development pressures.

Our plan aims to provide a more cohesive strategy for urban planning that results in a district architecture that:

  1. Fosters a talent district
  2. Promotes a comfortable and safe environment (heat and shade, stormwater management)
  3. Develops a high-quality public realm: Walkable, healthy, social and attractive
  4. Stays affordable, mixed, vibrant, creative
  5. Provides alternate modes of (micro)mobility
  6. Generates a strong district-wide character and builds on Wynwood’s sense of place
